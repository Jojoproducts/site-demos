Why grind it out?

Grinding is the easiest and most efficient way to remove a tree stump. Here is how it stacks up against the alternatives.

Digging it out

  • Dangerous and labor intensive
  • The stump still has to be moved and discarded
  • Leaves a much bigger hole

Grinding

  • Usually done in well under an hour
  • Chews the roots out to well below ground level
  • Leaves a controlled pile of small chips and dirt
  • Less disturbance to your surrounding landscaping
  • Stops a stump that is left behind from starting new growth

Chemicals

  • Can take many months or years to work
  • Most landfills will not accept whole stumps, so removal does not end the job
