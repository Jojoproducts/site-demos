Why we are careful with big trees

Tree removal is dangerous work that takes above-average physical ability and quick reactions. We do not recommend anyone try to take down a big tree themselves.

Climb and lower

The most common method. We climb, lower limbs by rope and section the tree down in small pieces.

Block the trunk

Where needed, the trunk is lowered to the ground in blocks so surrounding vegetation and structures stay undamaged.

Bring in the crane

For extra large trees, or a hazardous or dead tree that cannot take the stress of movement, a crane gives a controlled removal.
