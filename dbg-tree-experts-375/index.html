What we do

Five services, one local crew. Tell us what is in your yard and we will tell you what it needs.

Tree Removal

We only recommend taking a tree down when it is truly necessary: illness, or a tree that is an imminent danger to people or property. When it has to go, an experienced team handles it with the right tools and machinery.

Tree Trimming

Careful pruning that protects the tree's health. We take out dead, diseased and loose branches and growth that crosses or interferes, and we know which low limbs to cut. Usually done in the winter months.

Stump Grinding

A rotating blade takes the stump down well below ground level and turns it to chips you can use as mulch. Grinding is faster than full stump removal, and it leaves the roots in the ground.

Debris Clean-Up

We leave the property clean.

  • Raking
  • Debris hauled off
  • Walkways, driveways and roofs cleared
  • Log removal

Storm Clean-Up

Experience with all kinds of debris after a dangerous storm or tornado. Once it is safe, call us and we will plan the safest way to clear it.

Cut only what has to go

Honest advice

DBG is an eco-friendly company. If a tree can be saved with careful trimming, that is what we recommend. If illness or danger leaves no alternative, we will say so and take it down properly.

Cleaned up and disposed of right

After storm work, our trucks take debris to landfills and waste management or recycling centers, so each type of material is disposed of properly.

Storm hit? Call now.

We are available 24/7. Stay clear until the storm has passed, then call and we will plan the safest way to clear your property.
